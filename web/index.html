Kindred: find anime, manga, manhwa, manhua, novels, movies and series like the one you loved

Kindred is a free recommender that crosses media. Name one title you loved and it lists its closest kin in every medium: anime, anime films, manga, manhwa, manhua, light novels, movies and series. It covers 12,809 popular titles, runs entirely in your browser, needs no account and tracks nothing.

How it works

Fans of an anime and fans of its manga never rate things in the same place, so most sites cannot connect them. Kindred reads what each title is about and learns a small taste model from the recommendations that exist inside each medium. It was tested on a pre-registered benchmark and scores 0.217 NDCG@10 against 0.142 for the best baseline. Failed hypotheses and limits are published.
